## About

- Repo: [https://github.com/frycz/evalix](https://github.com/frycz/evalix)
- PyPI: [pypi.org/project/evalix](https://pypi.org/project/evalix/)

`evalix` is a small eval harness that tells you which cases your prompt change broke.

## Why

I created `evalix` because I was tired of building the same loops over and over again to test my prompts. Initially, it was supposed to be a tiny wrapper, but it was growing, I was adding more and more new options. This is what we have now - a pretty decent tool for evaluating prompts quality.
