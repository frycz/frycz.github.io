## About

- Repo: [https://github.com/frycz/capix](https://github.com/frycz/capix)
- PyPI: [pypi.org/project/capix](https://pypi.org/project/capix/)

`capix` is a thin, stateless wrapper around the Claude Messages API. Send a prompt, get text back.

## Why

I created `capix` because I wanted full control over what I send to the Claude Messages API, without having to `curl` the endpoints and build the payload by hand every time.
